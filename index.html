What's under the hood?

🏎️

Race Anywhere

Click the extension icon to drop a fully physics-enabled car onto any webpage. Drive over text, drift around images, and explore the web like never before.

🛠️

Track Builder

Open the toolbox to spawn walls, bumpers, ice patches, mud, and soccer balls. Build custom levels right on top of your favorite websites.

🤖

AI Bot Soccer

Spawn an AI opponent and drop down some goals to instantly turn Wikipedia, YouTube, or Reddit into a chaotic Rocket League-style soccer match.

🚁

Open World City

Press 'M' to open the massive, procedurally generated city map. Collect coins, complete quests, and survive up to a 5-star police chase with helicopters and tanks!
